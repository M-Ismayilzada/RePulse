What happens next

⚡ Strategic Roadmap

Expansion phases
Phase 1 · Native YOLOv8 + OpenCV verification
Rear-camera capture across a 3.2 second window, three 640×640 snapshots spaced 1 second apart, decoded with OpenCV and scored by an in-RAM YOLOv8 model running on our own container — no external API, no tokens.
Phase 2 · ESP32 smart-lock actuation
A successful verification makes the backend fire a background GET at the physical lid lock on the campus bin network.
Phase 3 · ADA 7-school leaderboard
SITE, SB, SPIA, LAW, SE, SAFS, and SDA compete in real time. Every valid drop is exactly +5 PTS.
Phase 4 · Campus-wide bin mesh
Expand verified bins across dorms, canteens, and faculty buildings with shared telemetry.
Phase 5 · City-scale recycling network
Export the RePulse protocol beyond ADA University as a municipal smart-waste layer.
